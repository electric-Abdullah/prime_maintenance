How to Use Admin Panel

� Projects Management:

  1. Fill in project details (title, category, location, description)
  2. Upload project photos (at least 1 required)
  3. Click "Add Project" to save
  4. Project will appear in the list below and on your website
  5. Click "Delete" to remove a project

💬 Testimonials Management:

  1. Fill in client name, title/role, and testimonial message
  2. Select a rating (1-5 stars)
  3. Click "Add Testimonial"
  4. Testimonial will appear on your website immediately
  5. Click delete button to remove a testimonial

📊 Google Sheets Integration:

  1. Create a public Google Sheet with your statistics
  2. Copy the sheet URL and paste it in the Google Sheets section
  3. Click "Test Connection" to verify
  4. Your live statistics will display on the website

Tip: All data is stored in Supabase database. Your content is safe and persistent.
